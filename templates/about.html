About

Onelink is a free to use online website dedicated to housing study tools and resources for year 12 students undertaking the HSC in New South Wales. It aims to provide students with a single place to store all their study resources.

OneLink works by allowing students to find and access all the study materials the internet has to offer without the hassle of needing to look for it themselves. This saves students time that they can put towards their studies.
